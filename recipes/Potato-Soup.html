Potato Recipes

Description

This recipe is in my head, but easy to make. My family loves homemade soups of all types.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 6 cups mashed cooked potatoes
  • 2 (14.5 ounce) cans chicken broth
  • ½ cup milk

Steps

  1. In a medium soup pot melt butter over low heat
  2. Saute onions until tender
  3. Stir in the mashed potatoes
  4. then slowly add the chicken broth
  5. Stirring, add milk (use more or less to achieve desired creaminess)
  6. Cook until heated through and season with salt and pepper to taste
